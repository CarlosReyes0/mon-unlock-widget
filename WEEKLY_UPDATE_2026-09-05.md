# Weekly Update – Open Paywall 🧠 Week #36

**Project:** Open Paywall  
**PRs merged since Sunday, August 30** (through September 5, 2026)  
**Live:** https://mon-unlock-widget-production.up.railway.app

Since last Sunday we shipped eleven updates. If an AI helper posts your article, readers now pay 50 cents by default, though you can still charge in MON (#42), and you can text that helper your title, price, teaser, and story from your phone — no forms or code (#43). The author name box starts empty so your phone won’t fill in your real name (#44), sign-in with email, Google, or a wallet works on iPhone (#45), photos and videos are checked before they go in so you can edit the story later without starting over (#46), and on iPhone the Add photo / video buttons stay on screen when the keyboard is open (#47). Writers can list a story on our Articles page so people pay once and read it here or on the writer’s own site (#48); that page was missing on the live site and we put it back (#49), stories on our site wouldn’t take payment and now they do — stories pasted on other sites still need the writer’s stamp (#50) — and the button now says “Pay 0.50 USDC” before you tap so nobody thinks it’s free (#51). We also removed leftover shop tabs on old demo pages that made people think they were browsing a store (#52).
