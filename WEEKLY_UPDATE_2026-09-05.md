# Weekly Update – Open Paywall 🧠 Week #36

**Project:** Open Paywall  
**Since Sunday, August 30**  
**Live:** https://mon-unlock-widget-production.up.railway.app

Since last Sunday we shipped ten updates.

If an AI helper posts your article, readers pay 50 cents. You can still charge in MON. You can text the helper your title, price, and story from your phone.

Signing in works on iPhone. Photos and videos are checked before they go in, and you can edit later without starting over. Add photo buttons stay on screen when the keyboard is open.

Writers can list a story on our Articles page. Readers pay once and can read it here or on the writer’s site. That page was missing, then payments didn’t work — both are fixed. The button now shows the price so nobody thinks the story is free. We also cleaned up old demo pages that looked like a store.
