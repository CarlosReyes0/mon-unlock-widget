# Weekly Update – Open Paywall 🧠 Week #36

**Project:** Open Paywall  
**PRs merged since Sunday, August 30** (through September 5, 2026)  
**Live:** https://mon-unlock-widget-production.up.railway.app

Since last Sunday we shipped ten updates.

If an AI helper posts your article, readers now pay 50 cents by default. You can still charge in MON if you prefer (#42). You can also text the helper your title, price, teaser, and story from your phone — no forms or code (#43).

Signing in with email, Google, or a wallet now works on iPhone (#45). Photos and videos are checked before they go into the story, so you can edit later without starting over (#46). On iPhone, the Add photo and Add video buttons stay on screen when the keyboard is open (#47).

Writers can list a story on our Articles page. Readers pay once and can read it here or on the writer’s own site (#48). That page was missing on the live site; we put it back (#49). Stories listed on our site can take payment again. If you paste the paywall on another site, you still need the writer’s approval stamp (#50). The button now says “Pay 0.50 USDC” before you tap, so nobody thinks the story is free (#51). We also cleaned up leftover shop tabs on old demo pages that made it look like a store (#52).
