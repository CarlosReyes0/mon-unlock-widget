Privacy

Open Paywall lets you follow a writer for free and pay only for the posts you read. This page covers that follow. Payments still go through the card checkout or a USDC transfer on Monad.

What we collect when you follow

  • Your email address, if you choose email follow. We store it in lowercase.
  • Your wallet address, if you follow with a connected wallet. No email is required for that.
  • When you followed, which page you used, and a hash of your IP address. We do not store the raw IP.
  • The consent line you saw, and the time you confirmed an email follow.

Email follow is double opt-in. We send one confirm message. You are not followed until you press Confirm on the page that link opens. A scanner that only opens the link does not confirm.

Who can see your email

The writer you follow can export their list, including your email, as a CSV. The follow form says so before you submit. Wallet-only follows are an address, not an email. We do not sell or import mailing lists.

New-post email is sent with Resend from mail.openpaywall.app when that mail is turned on. Until a postal address is configured, Open Paywall does not send new-post email.

Stopping email

Every new-post email has an unfollow link for that writer and a way to stop all Open Paywall emails. Both take effect immediately. The one-click unsubscribe header does the same thing. You can also unfollow from a browser that saved your follower token.

What this is not

Follow is not a paid membership. A paid membership, when a writer offers one, is separate and stays inside the paywall. Unfollowing does not refund a post you already unlocked.
